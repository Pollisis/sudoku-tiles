Help

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9. A number can appear only once in each.

Tap a cell, then a number tile to place it. Or pick a number first and tap the cells you want it in. Tap a number you’ve already placed to take it back.

The pencil button turns on notes. While it’s on, numbers go down as small marks in the cell. When you place a number, matching notes in its row, column and box are cleared for you (you can turn that off in Options).

Matching numbers light up with a green glow so you can see where they already sit. A wrong entry turns red and counts as a mistake. You can make as many as you need — this is a casual game — but a perfect game is one finished with no mistakes.

Undo takes back your last change. The restart button deals a fresh puzzle of the same difficulty.

Flash is a nearly full board, meant to be a quick warm-up. The others get steadily tougher, from Easy through Expert.

Score is points for each correct number, multiplied by the difficulty, plus a time bonus when you finish. A perfect game adds a little extra. A “run” is how many perfect games in a row you’ve done at that difficulty.

About

Sudoku Tiles is a cozy wooden-tile sudoku made for quiet evenings on the couch. It was built for Jessica and Stephen.

Every puzzle has one solution and can be finished by logic alone. There are no ads, and nothing to buy.

The look is original — aged parchment and hand-placed wood tiles — with a few wood colors to choose from in Options.
